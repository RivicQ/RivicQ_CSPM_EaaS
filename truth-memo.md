## Situation

- Community offers static CBOM/SBOM inventory, CycloneDX 1.6 export, and a deterministic policy gate.
- Enterprise adds SSO, server-side RBAC, tenant isolation, connectors, audit evidence, and control mappings.
- GitHub Pages is a labeled demo with no live scan API or customer data.

- The request-demo flow already supports enterprise demo, PoC, CBOM pilot, and partner conversations.

## What We Think
- The best first buyer is a security leader in a 50–1000 employee EU/DACH regulated or cryptography-heavy organization.
- The technical champion is an AppSec, Security Engineering, DevSecOps, PKI, or GRC lead.
- Audit evidence, customer security reviews, post-quantum readiness, and security hiring are the strongest triggers.
- A free Community CBOM pilot is the best first offer.

## What We Hope
- Buyers will authorize a source tree or fixture copy quickly.
- The pilot evidence will be useful enough to justify an Enterprise conversation.
- Sellbird can produce explainable, high-signal prospects from confirmed signals.

## Current Bottleneck
- customer-validation: no direct interviews or pipeline evidence are recorded in this workspace.

## Contradictions / Weak Assumptions
- Product maturity is stronger than commercial evidence.
- The buyer's owner, trigger, and willingness to authorize a source tree are still hypotheses.
- Live checkout, hosted pilot operations, retention, and pricing conversion are not proven.

## Main Contradiction
- RivicQ can produce strong technical evidence, but it does not yet have recorded customer evidence that this evidence changes buying behavior.

## Verdict
- validate-first

## One Next Move

## What Would Change My Mind
