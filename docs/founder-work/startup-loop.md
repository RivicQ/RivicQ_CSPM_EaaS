# Startup Loop

## Goal Capsule
**Customer:** Security leader in a 50–1000 employee EU/DACH regulated or cryptography-heavy organization.
**Problem:** They cannot produce a trustworthy cryptographic inventory for audits, customer reviews, or post-quantum planning.
**Current bottleneck:** customer-validation
**Success signal:** 5 target interviews confirm owner, workaround, urgency, and an authorized source tree for a CBOM pilot.

## Evidence Ledger
- E1. Workspace initialized on 2026-11-17; no direct customer interviews yet.
- E2. Product evidence: Community CLI, CBOM/SBOM, CycloneDX 1.6 export, policy gate, and labeled Pages demo (`README.md`, `docs/editions.md`).
- E3. Pilot evidence: controlled pilot is ready for static cryptographic inventory, with explicit non-claims (`docs/PILOT_READINESS.md`, `docs/CAPABILITIES.yaml`).
- E4. Sales evidence: request-demo intents include enterprise demo, PoC, CBOM pilot, and IBM Partner Plus (`web/src/pages/RequestDemo.tsx`).
- E5. GTM setup: Founder Skills installed; Sellbird configured for the ICP, buyer titles, hiring signals, and offer.

## Assumptions
- A1. A security leader in a regulated EU/DACH mid-market company owns the pain and can sponsor a pilot.
- A2. Audit, customer review, post-quantum planning, or security hiring creates enough urgency to test now.
- A3. A free, static CBOM pilot is a better first offer than a hosted SaaS demo.
- A4. Security, AppSec, DevSecOps, GRC, PKI, or cryptography hiring is a useful buying trigger.

## Decisions
- D1. Start uncertain moves with `co-founder`.
- D2. Focus first on the customer in C1, not individual developers or large enterprise procurement.
- D3. Use the free Community CLI and controlled CBOM pilot as the entry offer.
- D4. Use Sellbird only for explainable, confirmed signals and require approval before sending.
