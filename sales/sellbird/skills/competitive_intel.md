# RivicQ Competitive Boundary

Do not invent competitor comparisons from memory. RivicQ's differentiation must stay inside repository evidence.

## Safe differentiation

- One cryptographic intelligence engine across Community and Enterprise, with clear license boundaries.
- Static CBOM and SBOM inventory with CycloneDX 1.6 export and a deterministic policy gate for CI.
- A conservative pilot gate and capability manifest that say what is ready, limited, planned, and blocked.
- Enterprise control plane for SSO, server-side RBAC, tenant isolation, connectors, audit evidence, and compliance mappings.

## Unsafe claims

- Certifications from control mappings.
- IBM Quantum hardware results from Qiskit-aligned local scores.
- Trained AI from template or heuristic output.
- Silicon attestation or protected-key extraction.
- Live scanning or customer data storage on GitHub Pages.
- Real-time detection or continuous scanning in the current pilot.

## Discovery response

When a prospect names a competitor or incumbent, ask which part of the problem it actually solves: cryptographic inventory, SBOM, certificate/PKI ownership, policy gate evidence, SSO/RBAC, cloud connectors, or audit mapping. Compare only on the parts the prospect confirms and only with RivicQ capabilities marked ready or limited in docs/CAPABILITIES.yaml.
