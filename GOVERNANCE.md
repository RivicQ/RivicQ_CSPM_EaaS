# Governance

CryptoBOM SaaS is maintained by RivicQ GmbH. We welcome community contributions
under the Apache 2.0 license. Governance is currently:

- **Steering**: RivicQ core engineering team sets roadmap and release cadence.
- **Maintainers**: Repo maintainers review PRs and manage releases.
- **Contributors**: Any contributor can submit PRs; maintainers merge after
  review and passing CI.

If you are interested in becoming a maintainer, open an issue with your
contributions and experience; maintainers will review and nominate new
maintainers periodically.
